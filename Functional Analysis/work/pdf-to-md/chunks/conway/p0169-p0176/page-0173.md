What happens if G is only a semigroup? In this case $L _ { x }$ and $R _ { x }$ may not be isometries, so $\{ L _ { x } R _ { y } ; x , y \in G \}$ may not be noncontractive. However, there are measures for some semigroups that are invariant (see Exercise 7). For further reading see Greenleaf [1969].
