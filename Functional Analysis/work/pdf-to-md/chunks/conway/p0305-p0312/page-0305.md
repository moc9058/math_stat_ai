## §9. Invariant Subspaces for Normal Operators

Remember that we continue to assume that all Hilbert spaces are separable.

Every normal operator on a Hilbert space of dimension at least 2 has a nontrivial invariant subspace. This is an easy consequence of the Spectral Theorem. Indeed, if $N = \int z   d E ( z )$ $E ( \Delta ) \mathcal { H }$ is a reducing subspace for every Borel set ∆.
