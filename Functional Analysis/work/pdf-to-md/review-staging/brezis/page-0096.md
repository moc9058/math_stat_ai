<u>3.9</u> Let E be a Banach space; let $M \subset E$ be a linear subspace, and let $f _ { 0 } \in E ^ { \star }$ Prove that there exists some $g _ { 0 } \in M ^ { \perp }$ such that

$$
\inf_{g \in M^{\perp}} \| f_0 - g \| = \| f_0 - g_0 \|.

1. Use Theorem 1.12.
